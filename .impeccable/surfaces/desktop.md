# Desktop counter workspace

Mode: Operate. Native egui desktop on macOS and Windows; web/iOS/Android rendering guidance does not apply.

Source of truth: the user's POS Design System v0.1 in `design/` ("Calm counter"). The 2026-10-13 redesign replaced the earlier slate/green Counter Workspace and Noto-only typography. Implementation: `apps/pos-desktop/src/ui/` (tokens copied from `design/rust/tokens.rs`) plus `shell.rs` and the screen modules.

Keep: cart/payment left, products right; Thai/English; Light/Dark/System; Compact (mouse/keyboard) and Touch densities; offline SQLite/Diesel business behavior and every transaction invariant in AGENTS.md.

Direction: warm stone canvas with floating surfaces 10 points apart; one saffron primary per view, only for the money-moving or commit action; status colours always paired with text; blue focus ring distinct from accent; IBM Plex Sans Thai/Sans for text and IBM Plex Mono for every amount, right-aligned, never truncated.

Shell: top bar = workspace switcher (Alt 1–9, grouped daily work / manage shop; Activity is 8, Tables 9), shop context, Ctrl K command palette (pages, commands, products, receipts), offline and shift status pills (shift pill opens Shift & drawer), display popover (language, theme, density). Errors appear as a dismissible banner above the workspace; results of actions as toasts (undo for reversible actions, Ctrl Z).

Sell: Current bill / Held tabs; bill name; lines with qty pill, name, amount, accent options/notes; current line shows − qty + editor, note/options and remove; pinned summary (items total, amount due, method segmented, cash field, change), quick cash chips, XL pay button with full amount and F9. Right: category column with counts, scanner-focused search, product tiles (in-cart count, low/out/not-for-sale/options states). Products with options open a side sheet.

Management screens: Products = side nav (categories, saved views) + filter bar / bulk bar + table + inspector (sheet below 1200 points). History = receipts table + inspector. Reports = stat tiles + hourly chart + methods + top products. Shift = tabs (overview, count & close with steps, history). Settings = cards.

Window: resizable, minimum 1000×700 points; screens too small for that get a smaller UI zoom and a maximized window rather than a clipped pay button; statement pages centre at ≤ 1440 points; full screen is an optional device preference (F11).

Checks: native 1280×820 and 1000×700 content sizes, both themes, both densities, Thai and English; reproducible captures via `examples/seed_screens.rs` with `--page`, debug `--preview` and `--screenshot`. Screenshots are macOS evidence only. Physical touch hardware, Windows runtime and real barcode scanners remain unverified.

Risks: egui does not shape Thai with GPOS or segment Thai words; IBM Plex Sans Thai rendered acceptably at 12–34 points (rare `ฤๅ` falls back to Noto). Product names wrap to three lines on tiles with a full-name tooltip. Sell shows up to 500 products per category/search.

Tables and kitchen (schema 15): Floor = Sell's Tables tab (Products / Tables tabs once tables exist, `Alt 9` toggles): zones of free/busy cards (busy: Saffron Wash fill, selected border, total in Money type, guests · minutes, "unsent N" in warning text; the open table: 3 px saffron border + "Open"); tap → that table's bill loads on the left and the pane returns to Products; Counter / takeaway and Open kitchen screen · N in the floor header; move mode via More → Move to another table; guests via the "Table · N guests" caption; Held lists counter bills only. Table bills have two footer modes: ordering (default) is one sunken total line (items · SC/VAT/discount detail · total in Money), Send to kitchen and a primary Take payment ฿total (F9); Take payment opens the full payment section (`SellState.paying` = that cart id) with a ghost Back to ordering (Esc) and a warning when dishes are unsent; the kitchen button is hidden there. Adding a dish or switching bills returns to ordering. Counter bills keep the inline payment section. Sell with tables: full-width secondary "Send to kitchen (F10)" above the pay button showing new/cancel counts, Md on short windows; after sending, paying or holding a table bill the app shows the floor; clearing a bill the kitchen already has sends a cancellation instead of the undo toast. Kitchen screen: a second OS window (egui immediate viewport) for a kitchen monitor; top-aligned ticket cards oldest first, header minutes in warning from 10 and danger from 20 with the number as text, tap a line to tick it (strikethrough), Done bumps, Recently done → Recall; its actions never block the counter. Checks: Tables Light Thai Compact 1280×820 and Dark English Touch 1000×700; kitchen Light Thai and Dark English Touch; table bill Light Thai 1280×820 and Dark Thai Touch 1000×700 (bill lines scroll). Captures embed the kitchen window; a real second monitor is unverified.
