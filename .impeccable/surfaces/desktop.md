# Desktop counter workspace

Mode: Operate. Native egui desktop on macOS and Windows; web/iOS/Android rendering guidance does not apply.

Source of truth: the user's POS Design System v0.2 in `design/` ("Calm counter"; single theme: graphite chrome + paper panes). The 2026-10-13 redesign replaced the earlier slate/green Counter Workspace and Noto-only typography; the v0.2 adoption replaced Light/Dark/System with one palette, five accent presets and Soft/Square shape. Implementation: `apps/pos-desktop/src/ui/` (tokens copied from `design/rust/tokens.rs`) plus `chrome.rs`, `shell.rs` and the screen modules.

Keep: cart/payment left, products right; Thai/English; one theme with per-device accent (Saffron/Jade/Ocean/Plum/Ink, `accent.txt`) and shape (Soft/Square, `shape.txt`); Compact (mouse/keyboard) and Touch densities; offline SQLite/Diesel business behavior and every transaction invariant in AGENTS.md.

Direction: graphite chrome and canvas with paper panes (Soft: floating 6 points apart, radius 10; Square: edge to edge with 1 point splitters, corners capped at 3); one accent primary per view, only for the money-moving or commit action; status colours always paired with text; blue focus ring distinct from accent; IBM Plex Sans Thai/Sans for text and IBM Plex Mono for every amount, right-aligned, never truncated.

Shell (`chrome.rs`): OS title bar kept; workspace tab row (Home, Sell, History, Shift, Products, Reports, Settings = Alt 1–7; selected = chrome-hover fill + 2 point accent underline; Activity Alt 8 highlights Reports, Tables Alt 9 highlights Sell) with shop context and the Ctrl K command field; per-page toolbar of icon + label tools; status bar (staff, offline, shift, drawer cash, printer, accent, rounded/square, language, density, clock), every item clickable; the accent item opens the display popover (language, accent, corners). Panes have 28 point header strips (38 touch). Errors appear as a dismissible banner above the workspace; results of actions as toasts (undo for reversible actions, Ctrl Z).

Sell: bill | categories | products panes (touch: category chips with colour dots); Current bill / Held pane tabs; bill name; lines with qty pill, name, amount, accent options/notes; current line shows − qty + editor, note/options and remove; pinned summary and amount due, then two steps: Pay (F9) opens the payment panel in place of the lines (method, cash field, quick cash, change) whose confirm button states what is missing while disabled (grey, never faded accent). Category pane with colour dots and counts, scanner-focused search, product tiles with a category colour stripe (in-cart count, low/out/not-for-sale/options states). Products with options open a side sheet.

Home: shift card on chrome, Today pane, To do task cards, Held bills & open tables (three columns). Management screens: Products = toolbar actions + side nav (categories, saved views) + table + inspector (sheet below 1180 points). History = receipts table + inspector. Reports = toolbar period/export/Activity + quick periods + stat tiles + hourly chart + methods + top products. Shift = toolbar tabs (overview, count & close with steps, history). Settings = nav pane + content pane of raised cards.

Window: resizable, minimum 1000×700 points; screens too small for that get a smaller UI zoom and a maximized window rather than a clipped pay button; statement pages centre at ≤ 1440 points; full screen is an optional device preference (F11).

Checks: native 1280×820 and 1000×700 content sizes, both shapes, both densities, Soft and Square, Thai and English; reproducible captures via `examples/seed_screens.rs` with `--page`, debug `--preview` and `--screenshot`. Screenshots are macOS evidence only. Physical touch hardware, Windows runtime and real barcode scanners remain unverified.

Risks: egui does not shape Thai with GPOS or segment Thai words; IBM Plex Sans Thai rendered acceptably at 12–34 points (rare `ฤๅ` falls back to Noto). Product names wrap to three lines on tiles with a full-name tooltip. Sell shows up to 500 products per category/search.

Tables and kitchen (schema 15): Floor = Sell's Tables view (chrome segmented Products / Tables at the right of the Sell toolbar once tables exist, `Alt 9`): zones of table cards; free = dashed outline with name, seats and "Free"; seated = raised paper card with a 3 point status stripe (warning when dishes are unsent, else info), "Open · N min", total in Money and a status badge ("Not sent N" / "In kitchen"); the open table gets the 2 point border-selected outline. Tap → that table's bill loads on the left and the pane returns to Products; Counter / takeaway and Open kitchen screen · N in the floor header; move mode via More → Move to another table; guests via the "Table · N guests" caption; Held lists counter bills only. Table bills use the same two-step footer as counter bills: secondary Send to kitchen (F10, new/cancel counts) beside the primary Pay (F9); the payment panel warns when dishes are unsent. After sending, paying or holding a table bill the app shows the floor; clearing a bill the kitchen already has sends a cancellation instead of the undo toast. Kitchen screen: a second OS window (egui immediate viewport) for a kitchen monitor; top-aligned ticket cards oldest first, header minutes in warning from 10 and danger from 20 with the number as text, tap a line to tick it (strikethrough), Done bumps, Recently done → Recall; its actions never block the counter. Checks: Tables Thai Compact 1280×820 and English Touch 1000×700, Soft and Square; kitchen Thai and English Touch; table bill Thai 1280×820 and Thai Touch 1000×700 (bill lines scroll). Captures embed the kitchen window; a real second monitor is unverified.
