# Shop startup and daily workspace

Mode: Operate. Native Rust/egui desktop; not web, iOS or Android. Follows `design/` (POS Design System v0.1) and `desktop.md`.

First-run setup: centred card with shop name, Save & add products (primary) and Set up later (ghost). There is no shop type (removed 2026-10-13; `shop.json` keeps the old `kind` field for compatibility). The top bar shows only the setup label and display preferences during setup. A new real data directory shows setup; existing stores and Demo open Home. Skip persists defaults; setup persists the profile before opening Products.

Home: shop name and Bangkok date (Buddhist era in Thai); four stat tiles (today's gross, refunds, net sales labelled "not drawer cash", expected drawer cash); Shift & drawer card (status badge, expected cash, Open shift / Start / Continue selling primary, Count & close); Pending work (unfinished drawer count, backup older than 7 days or never, held bills with Open); shortcut buttons with Alt hints. Today's totals use `today_summary`, never the report date. Refreshes on entry and once per minute.

Settings layout: a SideNav (196 px, 220 px at 1100 and wider) lists seven sections and shows one at a time in a scrolling column (max 760 px): Shop & startup · Pricing & tax (pricing + VAT cards) · Tables & zones · Printing · การพิมพ์ (Printed documents + Receipt printer cards) · Staff & permissions · Language & display · Data & backup. A section with an unsaved draft shows a warning dot (tooltip "Unsaved changes"); drafts survive switching sections. Ctrl K finds sections by name ("Settings › …") once something is typed; the empty-tables state opens Tables & zones. Hints that point elsewhere use the section names (Settings → Shop & startup, → Staff & permissions).

Settings: Shop & startup card (name, Open directly to Sell switch, unsaved banner, Discard / Save); Language, theme & density card (apply immediately); Data & backup card (data folder, Create recovery backup, Export products CSV, backup-folder path, Inspect backup, reviewed counts/time/shop and Review recovery confirmation). Corrupt or future `shop.json` is never overwritten and makes the profile read-only with an explanation.

Staff (optional): when active staff exist, launch shows a lock screen (choose name, PIN pad or keyboard digits) that blocks pages, dialogs, the palette and global keys; sessions do not survive restart. The top bar shows the signed-in name chip with Lock / switch user (Ctrl L). Settings → Staff & permissions: start staff sign-in (creates the first owner), owners add/edit name, role (chips for any role, with a summary of its Allowed / Approval / Denied permissions), new PIN twice and active switch; others see the roster read-only. Roles list (people count, "Approves" badge, Edit) and Add role; the role dialog has the name (built-in names fixed and localized), a three-way segmented control per permission (manage staff hidden, owner only), "May approve others", "Discount without approval %" and Delete role for custom roles, and scrolls on short or touch windows. Shop, pricing, VAT and printer cards are editable only when the role allows Settings. Recovery actions are available for Allow or Approval, and the worker checks Settings before any cart flush or recovery work. Reports shows a locked state unless the role allows Reports.

Pricing card (second, after the shop card; Settings permission): service charge %, cash rounding Off / 0.25 / 0.50 / 1 baht and direction (down in the customer's favour by default, or nearest), with help that rounding is cash-only, VAT is taken from the amount paid, and to confirm with an accountant. When service is on, the product editor shows a Service charge applies switch.

VAT card (Settings permission): registered switch, registered name, tax ID with check-digit validation, head office/branch, address, rate, POS number and product prices include VAT / exclude VAT (added on top), with help text that abbreviated tax invoices from a POS need Revenue Department approval first (ภ.พ.06) and that the app does not file returns. Changes apply to receipts paid afterwards only.

Navigation: workspace switcher and Ctrl K replace the old row of page buttons; every workspace is also reachable by Alt 1–9. Icon-only controls (display preferences, line actions) carry accessible names and tooltips.

Behavior: draft profile and product edits survive navigation; closing the window warns before discarding them. The open bill autosaves, so closing never loses it. Native macOS Quit/Cmd+Q bypasses the window-close prompt.

Checks: 1280×820 and 1000×700, Light/Dark, Thai/English, Compact/Touch; first-run completion/skip, existing DB, preference restart, report-date isolation. No physical-touch or Windows claims.

Recovery confirmation is destructive styling, never saffron. Save/discard drafts and hold/clear the current cart first. On preparation, the worker stops and a dedicated screen gives the restorable safety-backup path and Close app; activation precedes settings/SQLite on next launch. Printer/display preferences stay device-local.

Tables & zones card (Settings permission): list of zones and tables with Add table and an add/edit dialog (name auto-incremented from the last table, zone with chips for existing zones, seats, active). Switching off a table with an open bill and a duplicate active name in the same zone are refused with an inline error. Once tables exist the product editor shows "Goes to the kitchen" and the Receipt printer card shows Kitchen tickets (Off / Receipt printer / Kitchen printer with its own connection and paper).

Printed documents card (Settings permission): Receipt / Check bill / Kitchen ticket tabs, controls left and a live paper preview right of exactly what prints (latest sale or a sample; open or held bill; latest kitchen ticket or a sample). Title, header/footer lines (≤8 lines, ≤400 characters; title ≤60), logo (paste a PNG/JPEG path or drop it on the window; stored as `receipt-logo.png` and included in recovery backups), text size, language and show/hide switches. Defaults / Discard / Save; unsaved edits stay a draft. Templates never remove legally required VAT receipt or tax-invoice lines, and kitchen notes/cancellations always print. Templates live in `shop.json` and never rewrite stored receipts (reprints use the current template).

Development round (2026-10-14): Data & backup also has an opt-in Automatic backups card (Bangkok daily / after closing a shift, keep 3/7/14/30, Save/Discard/Back up now). It uses neutral buttons and scrolls with the section; dirty policy participates in navigation/recovery/close guards. Home counts the last verified automatic snapshot alongside manual backups. Staff & permissions starts with a device-local Lock when idle card (Off/1/5/10/15/30 min). Native input resets its monotonic timer, background work does not. Manual and idle lock hide all workspace dialogs and kitchen contents, clear PIN/approval and pending confirmations, preserve business drafts/cart/cash input, and require a fresh sign-in. Retained settings/staff editors remain permission-gated for the next actor. Both device policies stay on the destination device during recovery and are excluded from portable backup bundles.
